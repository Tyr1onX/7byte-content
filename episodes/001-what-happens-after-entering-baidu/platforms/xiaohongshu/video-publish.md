# Xiaohongshu Video Experiment — EP001

## Purpose

Use the already-frozen 7BYTE short-video master as a low-cost Xiaohongshu video-note experiment.

This intentionally reuses the same topic as the already-published EP001 image carousel. The goal is to keep the topic roughly controlled and compare **content format** rather than immediately changing both topic and format at the same time.

## Experiment design

Compare with the EP001 image-carousel note.

Record at 24h / 72h / 7d:

- exposure / impressions
- video plays / viewers
- cover click performance if available
- average watch time
- completion rate if available
- likes
- saves
- comments
- shares
- profile visits
- new follows
- search traffic / query terms if available

Interpretation rule: this is one format-control sample, not enough to conclude that Xiaohongshu prefers video or images. Accumulate more samples before changing the platform strategy.
